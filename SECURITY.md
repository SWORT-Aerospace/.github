# Security policy

This policy covers every public repository in the SWORT-Aerospace organization
that does not publish its own. The policy for the website itself is at
[swort.net/security](https://swort.net/security).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **contact@swort.net** with the subject line `SECURITY`.

Include the repository and commit, what you did, what you observed and what you
expected. A proof of concept is welcome.

**Do not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security
report.

We aim to acknowledge within a few business days. SWORT is a small team, so we
will not promise a fix window we cannot meet. We will tell you where things
stand and what we intend to do.

## Controlled information

Do not include classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information or
export-controlled technical data in a report. If a finding involves such
material, say so in a short first email and SWORT will arrange an appropriate
channel.

## Please do not

- Run denial-of-service, stress or traffic-flooding tests
- Use social engineering or phishing, or target SWORT people
- Attempt physical access
- Do destructive testing, persistence, data exfiltration or lateral movement

Collect the minimum evidence needed to demonstrate the issue. If you come across
personal data, stop, do not keep it, and say so in your report.

## No bounty

SWORT does not run a paid bounty programme. We will credit you if you want to be
credited.
